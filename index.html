    Controls
    Move
    A D
    Jump
    Space, again in the air to double jump. Tap for a short hop.
    Down
    Hold S to crouch: smaller target, low attacks. Tap S twice to drop through a thin platform; in the air, fall fast.
    Block
    Hold Q or Shift (stops melee, not bullets). Let go just as a hit lands to parry and stun the attacker; crouch-block for low hits.
    Dodge
    F for half your stamina bar (the thin white line under your feet; a full bar is two dashes): a quick dash the way you hold, or backwards if you hold nothing. At the start of it bullets, melee and explosions miss you (spikes and the storm don't). Stamina also pays for shots fired crouched (with none left, crouching stops steadying your aim) and for attacking on the run (heavier weapons cost more; without enough, you stop to attack).
    Hide
    Stand in a doorway and press W. Bullets fly past you, but you can't move or shoot, and melee still hits. W, S, jump or move to come out.
    Pick up
    E on a weapon, gun or grenade lying on the floor (something new turns up every few seconds).
    Melee weapons
    Otaku Katana (light, fast; swing into bullets to send them back at the pointer) · Bolívar's Sword (heavy, balanced) · La Chola (heavy, slow, huge push; tap to swing, hold to throw it, and then it's gone) · Keyboard (light; every hit is a word, and the word decides how hard it hits). Also: Knife (light; double damage from behind; a fast, flat throw) · Machete (light, wide; every third hit in a row launches) · Nodachi (light, long; hold to charge a dash slash that launches) · Trident (takes your gun's place; tap to thrust, hold to throw it on its chain: a catch is stunned and reeled in, a miss leaves you standing there until it's back) · Shovel (heavy; swung from below, launches straight up) · Nail Bat (heavy; a home run, flat and far) · Cleaver (heavy, short, brutal) · War Hammer (heavy, slowest, biggest launch) · Bone Axe (heavy; goes through blocks). Light weapons never launch; heavy ones do. Hold the melee button to throw a light weapon: it pushes hard, and lands where it stops for you to go get back (the nodachi charges instead).
    Shoot
    Left click, toward the mouse. You start with a 9mm; pick up others. It reloads by itself (or R). Out of ammo, shooting throws the gun; with no gun, it punches. Light guns (9mm, SMG, Assault Rifle, Minigun) only add damage; heavy guns (Revolver, Shotgun, Star Blaster) push and stun; special guns (Sniper, Rocket Launcher) hit just as hard, held at the shoulder, but carry little ammo. A headshot stuns with any gun and hurts more. The weapon you used last is in your hand; the other rests on your back.
    Grenades
    Middle click or G: hold to cook it and aim, let go to throw (hold too long and it goes off in your hand). Carry two; the mouse wheel (or Z) picks which one goes next. Frag (big push) · Fire (big damage, sets you burning) · Smoke (a cloud nobody sees into) · Poison (a cloud that slowly hurts) · Warp (teleports you where it lands; hit someone and you swap places).
    Melee
    Right click, toward the mouse (hold to wind up a throw). Aim up for an uppercut.
    Controller
    Left stick moves, right stick aims. RT shoot, LT melee, A jump, B dodge, LB block, RB grenade, X reload, Y pick up, D-pad ↑ hide, ↓ crouch, → next grenade, Start menu. Change any of it in the menu (Esc → Controller). In Solo a controller works alongside the keyboard and mouse; in Same screen each controller is a player.
    Menu
    Esc (or Start): volume, your character, and key and button bindings. While playing, the mouse stays inside the game; the menu frees it.
    Same screen
    2 to 4 players on one screen: one on keyboard + mouse, the rest on controllers (or all on controllers). Choose the weapons in play, lives, the storm's pace, best of 1, 3 or 5 rounds, and bots.
    The arena
    Solid cover stops bullets; thin platforms don't. Barrels explode. Spikes hurt. After 45 s the storm closes in: its pale edge hurts, and its deep-red wall is the new edge of the stage. Get launched into it and you lose a life.
